F1 2021 Abu Dhabi GP

Author:Symply Formula

With just a few laps to go, a crash by Nicholas Latifi brought out the safety car.

Under the safety car, Verstappen pitted for fresh soft tyres — Hamilton did not pit, staying out to keep the lead.

Crucially: only some of the lapped cars (those between Hamilton and Verstappen) were allowed to “un-lap” themselves before the race was restarted — not all, as many fans and critics expected under standard practice.

Immediately after the “un-lapping,” the race was restarted for a final one-lap sprint. On that lap, with his fresh tyres, Verstappen overtook Hamilton and won — thus clinching his first Drivers’ Championship.
